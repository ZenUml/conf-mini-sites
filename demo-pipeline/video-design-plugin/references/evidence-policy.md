# Mini Sites evidence policy

- Every factual product claim must cite a repository source or a captured
  observation from the development stack.
- The primary launch story mirrors `tests/e2e/ui/full-flow.spec.ts` and uses the
  checked-in multi-file sample bundle under `tests/e2e/fixtures/sample-bundle/`.
- The observable product outcome for the first fixture is the rendered text
  `Mini-Site is live` after publication.
- Capture manifests must name the story, environment, browser, dimensions,
  source media hash, and action-event file.
- Dailies must distinguish a usable shot, a retake, and an unproven claim.
- Generated images, video, or audio must be marked generated and cannot fulfill
  a product-evidence requirement.
- QC must trace each spoken or on-screen claim to the approved script and its
  evidence. A missing or contradictory trace is a blocking error.
- Delivery manifests must include hashes and media probes for the exact master.
