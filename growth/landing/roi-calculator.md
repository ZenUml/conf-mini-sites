## Hero

Turn a formula in your document into something your team can actually use.

Your Cloud Migration Proposal has an ROI calculation buried in a paragraph or bolted on as a linked spreadsheet. Embed a live ROI calculator directly on the Confluence page instead — readers change the numbers and see payback period, savings, and 3-year ROI update instantly, without leaving the doc.

## Problem

Right now, when a stakeholder wants to test their own numbers against your migration business case, they have to open Excel, hunt for the right linked spreadsheet, or ask you to re-run the math with different assumptions. Each of those is a context switch that costs momentum — and most readers never make the switch at all. They skim the static numbers you gave them, or they skip the ROI section entirely. The formula that should be the most persuasive part of the proposal ends up the least interacted with.

## Demo

Try the working calculator: [`growth/demos/roi-calculator/index.html`](../demos/roi-calculator/index.html)

Enter current infrastructure cost, migration cost, and expected annual savings — payback period, annual savings, and 3-year ROI recalculate live as you type or drag the sliders. No page reload, no spreadsheet.

## How it works

1. **Create** — Build (or generate, see the Prompt section) a small self-contained HTML/CSS/JS bundle like the one in the demo above.
2. **Upload** — Add the bundle's files to the Mini Sites macro on your Confluence page.
3. **Publish** — The macro serves the bundle live, inline, on the page — readers interact with it right where the proposal lives.
