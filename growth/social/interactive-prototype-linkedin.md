# LinkedIn Post Draft — Interactive Prototype on Confluence

We kept watching the same thing happen in PRDs: someone builds a working prototype with Claude or v0,
then pastes a screenshot of it into the doc. A screenshot. Of something that runs.

The reviewer can't click anything. They ask "what happens when I press this button" — a question the
prototype already answers, if only they could touch it. So we built Mini Sites to fix exactly that.

Now you build your clickable prototype with whatever AI tool you already use — Claude, ChatGPT, v0,
Lovable, doesn't matter — export it as a plain HTML/CSS/JS bundle, and publish it straight into the
Confluence page your team is already reading. No external link to babysit, no separate login, no iframe
pointing at some sandbox that'll 404 in six months. It just runs, live, inline, exactly where the
decision is being made.

We built a small example ourselves: a feature-prioritization tool where you drag ideas across an
Impact/Effort matrix and vote on what ships next. Took about ten minutes end to end, prompt to published
page. That's the whole pitch — don't explain the prototype, let your team try it.
